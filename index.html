How opening works

  1. Pick the accountStar your top 2 from the Compare section, then read the catch on each.
  2. Fill the form in the bank's appName, address, PAN, Aadhaar number. Ten minutes, no branch visit for digital accounts.
  3. Video KYC — a 2-minute callShow your PAN and face on camera. The bank verifies you on the spot; no paperwork to courier.
  4. Set a nomineeName one person who can access the money if something happens to you. It takes 30 seconds — do not skip it.
Video KYC vs branch KYC: video KYC is accepted by RBI and is the fastest route. Branch KYC is the same account, just slower — pick it only if your Aadhaar phone number is not active.
